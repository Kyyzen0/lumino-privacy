Lumino Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026 · App covered: Lumino, for Android (io.github.kyyzen0.lumino)

In short

Lumino works entirely offline. The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no audience-measurement tool and no built-in third-party service.

Who publishes Lumino?

Lumino is published by Kyyzen0. For any question about this policy: audryk.chesse@icloud.com.

What Lumino stores on your phone

To work, Lumino keeps some game information on your device only, in the app's private space, which other apps cannot access:

  • your best score and your game statistics (number of games, total score, best combo, lines cleared, play time, records);
  • your settings (sound, vibration, animations);
  • whether you have already seen the tutorial;
  • your game in progress, so you can resume it later.

This information contains no name, no address, no phone identifier and no data that could identify you. Lumino never sends it. The publisher has no access to it.

Data collected or transmitted

None. Lumino does not even ask for permission to access the Internet: it has technically no way to send data. It does not use the advertising ID, location, contacts, camera, microphone or your phone's storage.

Permissions requested

Just one: vibration (haptic feedback during play). This is a basic permission, granted automatically at installation, that gives access to no data. You can turn vibration off in "Settings" or in Android's settings.

Third-party services

Lumino includes no third-party service for advertising, analytics, tracking, social networks or crash reporting. It only uses technical libraries provided by Google (AndroidX) and JetBrains (Kotlin), which run the interface on your phone and do not communicate with any server.

Android automatic backup

If you have turned it on in your phone's settings, Android may copy some app data to your Google account (automatic backup) so you can restore it on a new device. For Lumino, this copy covers only your settings, statistics and records, not your game in progress. This backup is handled by Android and Google, for you alone: Lumino's publisher has no access to it. You can turn it off or delete these backups in your Google account settings. See Google's privacy policy.

Google Play

Lumino is distributed through Google Play. When you install it, Google processes certain data under its own privacy policy. Google may give the publisher aggregated statistics (for example the number of installs) and, for users who chose to share them with Google, anonymized crash reports. This information does not identify any player.

Children

Lumino does not collect any personal data, whatever the player's age.

How long is data kept? How can I delete it?

Game data stays on your phone for as long as you keep the app. You can:

  • erase your statistics and records: "Settings › Reset statistics";
  • delete all the data on your phone: uninstall Lumino, or clear its storage in Android's settings ("Apps › Lumino › Storage").

Note on Android's automatic backup. If it is turned on, a copy of your settings, statistics and records may remain in your Google account after you uninstall, and Android may restore it if you reinstall Lumino. To avoid this, delete that backup in your Google account settings (or turn backup off in Android's settings) before reinstalling the app.

Your rights

Since the publisher holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on their side. You stay in control of your game data: it is on your device, and you can erase it at any time as described above, including the copy that Android's backup may hold, which stays under your control in your Google account. For any question, write to the contact address given above.

Changes to this policy

If Lumino changes in a way that affects the above (for example by adding advertising, accounts or an online leaderboard), this policy will be updated before the new version is released, and the date at the top of the page will be changed.
